A band plays songs it wrote together: each part was made in answer to the others. Drag a player forward to hear more of them, back to hear less, sideways to place them; drag a resting player onto the stage and they join the song and answer the others. Double-click to encourage, shift and double-click to discourage. The dial on the right is your mood; hold it low and the player you like least answers the others again, live. Between songs they write more from what you approved.
